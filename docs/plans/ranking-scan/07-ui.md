# Ranking scan · 07 · Interface

## Rankings → Ranking now (`?tab=now`)

**Header row:**
- the last scan (date, status) and the next automatic scan;
- **Scan now** (managers; "about $0.15 · N of 4 left this month") and **Export CSV**.

**Source chips:**
- **DataForSEO:** "N keywords · data from <date>", or "No data for this site yet", or "Skipped: budget used up".
- **Search Console:** "N searches · <from>–<to>", or "Not connected · Connect" (a link to Connections), or "Turn on Zernio's Analytics add-on".
- **Business Profile:** "N searches", or "Not connected".

**Strip** (filters in the URL `?v=`): Keep · Quick wins · Brand · Watch · Not useful · Lost since last scan. Toggles: "Not tracked yet" (`?untracked=1`) and source (`?src=dataforseo|search_console|google_business|several`). Plus text search (`?q=`).

**Table** (500 rows a page, sorted by score):

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| Select | For bulk actions |
| Keyword | Brand tag; "New" tag; "Tracked" tag |
| **Source** | DataForSEO · Search Console · Business Profile badges |
| Position | Rounded; hover shows each source's position, the previous scan and our city check |
| Change | Up or down against the last scan |
| Searches a month | City (national ÷ when estimated) |
| Clicks · Impressions | 3 months |
| CTR | With the rarely-clicked flag |
| Page | Shortened, links out |
| Verdict | Tag; hover lists the reasons |

**Actions:**
- **Track** (bulk) and **Track all Keep** (only untracked; stops at the keyword limit with a clear message).
- A row opens a **detail sheet**: each source's numbers, the reasons, positions across the client's last 6 scans, links to the page and DataForSEO's `check_url`.

**Phones:** the table becomes cards.

**Empty states:**
- no website and nothing connected: "Add the website or connect Search Console";
- never scanned: explains the sources plus Scan now;
- running: progress by step, refreshing every 15 seconds.

## Keywords tab

- **"Ranking well":** a badge on tracked keywords the latest scan judged Keep. Weak reasons are removed, and a note explains them (from the scan's reasons).
- **Weak count:** the header's "N weak" and the weak filter count only keywords that are still weak.
- **Swaps:** the Research tab's suggested swaps never offer to replace Keep or Quick-win keywords.
- **Dialogs:** Replace and Stop tracking show "This keyword ranks #3 and brought 41 clicks in 3 months" first.

## Settings tab: Ranking scan

- Monthly scan on/off, and the next date.
- The Search Console site (when several are connected), or "Connect Search Console".
- "Only searches from <country>".
- Brand terms (chips; the domain stem and the client name are always used).

## Elsewhere

- **Research tab:** the source filter gains Search Console and Business Profile. Step 1 reuses the latest scan's DataForSEO rows when the scan is under 14 days old (no second payment).
- **Client Overview:** the visibility panel line "Ranks for N keywords · M on page 1 · K quick wins" (from the latest scan's counts).
- **Bell:** the `rank_scan_finished` wording and a link.

## Downloads (2026-11-27)

Every Rankings keyword list (Keywords, Ranking now and its Lost view, Find better keywords and Dismissed) has a **Download CSV** button (`components/download-csv.tsx`). With a filter or search on, it offers the keywords shown or all of them. Columns and labels are built in `src/features/rankings/csv.ts` on top of `src/lib/csv.ts` (BOM, CRLF, formula guard). Checks: `scripts/rankings-csv-check.ts`.
