# Content · 08 · Interface

**Routes:**
- `/clients/[slug]/content`: tabs through `?tab=`.
- `/clients/[slug]/content/articles/[id]`: the editor; `new` creates the article on its first save.

## Page

- **Header:** New article, New post (writers and up).
- **Notices:** Zernio isn't set up; nothing is connected yet.
- **Month strip:** articles of the target, Google posts of the target, scheduled, needs attention (each one links to the matching list).
- **Tabs:** Calendar · Posts · Articles · Media · Settings.

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| Calendar | Month or week (`?view=week`, `?d=` anchor), Sunday weeks, in the client's time zone. Posts and articles as chips (status dot, time, platform logos or an article icon). A "+" on future days starts a post on that day. Phones get an agenda list |
| Posts | Filters: All, Drafts, Scheduled, Published, Needs attention (`?status=`). Rows: when, text, accounts (logo + status dot), status. Opening a row opens the composer |
| Articles | Table: title (+ last error), keyword, words, status, date, SEO fields (To paste / Pasted), link |
| Media | The library (`05-media.md`) |
| Settings | Time zone, Google button link, post signature, WordPress site and blog, default author, default tags. Only admins and managers can save |

## Editor

**Writing area:**
- Tiptap with headings 2–3, bold, italic, links, lists, quotes, dividers, images (with alt text), and undo/redo.
- Shows the word count and reading time.
- Autosaves every 10 s while there are changes, and warns before leaving with unsaved changes.

**Side panel:**
- **Publish:** date and time; Publish now, Schedule, Unschedule; or Update on WordPress and Unpublish; delete.
- **Paste the SEO fields:** after publishing.
- **Search appearance:** SEO title 60 and meta description 155 counters, with a Google-style preview.
- **Details:** slug, focus keyword (from tracked keywords, or typed), excerpt, tags, topic, author number.
- **Featured image.**
- **Before you publish:** the checklist (tips only).

## Permissions

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Admin, manager | Everything, plus settings and connecting WordPress |
| Writer | Write, schedule, publish, retry and upload on clients in their tasks (RLS) |
| Viewer | Read only |
| VA | Doesn't see Content (navigation) |

## Design

- Tokens only; `StatusTag` and `StatusDot` for every status.
- Dialogs use the app's alert dialog (`ConfirmProvider`).
- Dark mode follows the tokens.
- Phone width: the calendar becomes an agenda, the composer sheet goes full width, the editor's side panel stacks under the text.
